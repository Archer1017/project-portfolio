# Public static edition

This edition provides local climbing records, manual photo route marking and training management. Cloud image analysis is disabled and no server credentials are included.

Scene photography is from the original project assets, not the portfolio owner's personal photographs:

- `images/climb-hero.jpg`: [Unsplash](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1522163182402-834f871fd851)
- `images/climb-rock.jpg`: [Pexels 449609](https://www.pexels.com/photo/449609/)
- `images/climb-boulder.jpg`: [Pexels 3077882](https://www.pexels.com/photo/3077882/)

The images remain subject to their respective source licenses. They do not document the sample route locations.

The bundled application uses React, Radix UI, Lucide and DM Sans. Their upstream license notices are retained in `licenses/`.
